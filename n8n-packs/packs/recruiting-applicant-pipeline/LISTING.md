# Listing: Recruiting Applicant Pipeline

**Title:** n8n Recruiting Pipeline Automation | Application Intake, Hiring-Manager Routing, Candidate Emails | Google Sheets + Gmail

**Price tests:** Lite $39 / Pro $59

**Description:**

Every applicant acknowledged and routed, every status change communicated, no ATS subscription. This n8n pack gives you 5 importable workflows for small businesses hiring without an applicant-tracking system.

INCLUDED
- Application Intake
- Update Applicant Status
- Applicants Waiting For Review
- Weekly Hiring Report
- Error alert workflow
- Google Sheets header templates
- Step-by-step README and sample requests
- Built-in dry-run mode so you can test without sending anything

GOOD TO KNOW
- A HUMAN decides every candidate's status. The pack never scores, ranks or rejects anyone automatically; a rejection email is sent only after you set the status to `rejected`.
- Hiring regulations (equal opportunity, anti-discrimination, data retention, consent for storing applicant data) are your responsibility. Do not collect sensitive information (age, health, protected characteristics) in your application form. Not legal advice.
- Role options and hiring-manager routing are edited in the Config (`flow.fields` role options and `hiring_managers`).
- Applicant data lives in your Google Sheet. Restrict who can open it and delete old applicants on a schedule that matches your policy.

REQUIREMENTS: n8n (self-hosted or Cloud) and a Google account. Tested on n8n 2.42.5. This is a template, not a managed service.

**Do not claim:** sales, reviews, guaranteed results, endorsement by n8n/Google/Stripe/Shopify/Anthropic, or that it was tested with live accounts (it was not).
