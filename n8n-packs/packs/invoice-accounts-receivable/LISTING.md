# Listing: Invoice & Accounts Receivable

**Title:** n8n Invoice Payment Reminder Automation | Overdue Invoice Follow-Up, Paid Notices, AR Reports | Google Sheets + Gmail

**Price tests:** Lite $39 / Pro $59

**Description:**

Polite, consistent payment reminders and a weekly overdue list, without chasing clients by hand. This n8n pack gives you 6 importable workflows for freelancers, agencies and small service businesses that invoice clients.

INCLUDED
- Log New Invoice
- Payment Reminders
- Mark Invoice Paid
- Weekly Overdue Report
- Monthly Receivables Summary
- Error alert workflow
- Google Sheets header templates
- Step-by-step README and sample requests
- Built-in dry-run mode so you can test without sending anything

GOOD TO KNOW
- This pack does not create invoices or collect payments. Keep using your invoicing tool, then log each invoice with the `invoice-new` webhook (Zapier/Make can send it) or paste rows into the sheet.
- Reminders stop when you mark the invoice paid (`invoice-paid` webhook or set status to `paid` in the sheet).
- Payment reminders are transactional and have no STOP footer. The tone is courteous. Review wording for your jurisdiction; debt-collection rules can apply, and this is not legal advice or a collections service.
- Set `late_fee_text` in the Config only if a late fee is in your signed agreement.

REQUIREMENTS: n8n (self-hosted or Cloud) and a Google account. Tested on n8n 2.42.5. This is a template, not a managed service.
