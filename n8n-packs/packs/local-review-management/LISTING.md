# Listing: Local Business Review Management

**Title:** n8n Local Business Review & Feedback Automation | Review Requests, Private Feedback Form, Low-Rating Alerts

**Price tests:** Lite $29 / Pro $39

**Description:**

Ask every customer for a review the same way, and hear about unhappy customers the same day. This n8n pack gives you 5 importable workflows for local service businesses (cleaners, trades, salons, clinics, detailers).

INCLUDED
- Log A Completed Job
- Review Requests
- Private Feedback Form
- Weekly Reputation Report
- Error alert workflow
- Google Sheets header templates
- Step-by-step README and sample requests
- Built-in dry-run mode so you can test without sending anything

GOOD TO KNOW
- NO REVIEW GATING. Google, Yelp and Facebook prohibit sending only happy customers to the review site. This pack sends the review link to EVERY customer, and the private feedback form's thank-you also includes the public review link for every rating.
- Low ratings alert you so you can follow up personally. The pack never writes or posts replies for you.
- Mark `reviewed` = `yes` in the Customers sheet when a customer reviews, to stop reminders. `opted_out` = `yes` after a STOP reply.
- Check each platform's current rules about soliciting reviews; they change.

REQUIREMENTS: n8n (self-hosted or Cloud) and a Google account. Tested on n8n 2.42.5. This is a template, not a managed service.
