# Listing: Newsletter Research & Publishing

**Title:** n8n Newsletter Research Automation | RSS Story Collection, Dedupe, Editor Approval, Weekly Draft | Google Sheets + Gmail

**Price tests:** Lite $29 / Pro $49

**Description:**

Collect, filter and organize the week's stories automatically, then get a ready-to-edit draft. This n8n pack gives you 5 importable workflows for solo newsletter operators and small editorial teams.

INCLUDED
- Collect Stories
- Approve Or Reject Story
- Stories Waiting For Review
- Build Newsletter Draft
- Error alert workflow
- Google Sheets header templates
- Step-by-step README and sample requests
- Built-in dry-run mode so you can test without sending anything

GOOD TO KNOW
- Feeds, keywords and limits are set in the Config node. Replace the example feeds with your own.
- Stories keep only the title, link, source and a short (300 character) plain-text snippet from the feed, with a link back to the source. Check each source's terms before republishing excerpts, and write your own summaries where required.
- The weekly workflow creates a DRAFT email in the editor's Gmail. It never sends to subscribers. Paste the draft into your email platform (Substack, Beehiiv, Mailchimp...) and add your unsubscribe link.
- No AI summarization is used. Summaries are the feed's own text, shortened.

REQUIREMENTS: n8n (self-hosted or Cloud) and a Google account. Tested on n8n 2.42.5. This is a template, not a managed service.
