# Listing: Real Estate Lead Response

**Title:** n8n Real Estate Lead Response Automation | Instant Reply, Nurture Emails, Viewing Confirmations | Google Sheets + Gmail

**Price tests:** Lite $29 / Pro $49

**Description:**

Answer every lead in seconds and keep nurturing the ones who aren't ready yet. This n8n pack gives you 6 importable workflows for independent real estate agents and small teams.

INCLUDED
- Lead Intake And Instant Reply
- Lead Nurture Sequence
- Viewing Scheduled
- Leads Waiting For First Contact
- Weekly Lead Report
- Error alert workflow
- Google Sheets header templates
- Step-by-step README and sample requests
- Built-in dry-run mode so you can test without sending anything

GOOD TO KNOW
- Messages are neutral and informational. You are responsible for compliance with fair housing, CAN-SPAM, TCPA and your brokerage's rules. Review every template before using it.
- Email only. No SMS (SMS needs separate consent and carrier registration).
- Your Leads sheet is the CRM. Export it to your real CRM, or POST new leads to its API from the intake webhook.
- When someone replies STOP, set `opted_out` to `yes`; nurture emails then stop.

REQUIREMENTS: n8n (self-hosted or Cloud) and a Google account. Tested on n8n 2.42.5. This is a template, not a managed service.

**Do not claim:** sales, reviews, guaranteed results, endorsement by n8n/Google/Stripe/Shopify/Anthropic, or that it was tested with live accounts (it was not).
